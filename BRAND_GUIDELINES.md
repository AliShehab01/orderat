# Orderat brand guidelines

## Brand promise

**Orders clear. Day calm.**  
**طلبات واضحة. يوم أهدأ.**

Orderat behaves like a reliable operations partner for small sellers. It is clear about what happened, calm when something needs attention, and explicit about which actions require the owner’s approval.

## Typography

Use **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** for Arabic and **IBM Plex Sans** for English.

- Regular `400`: body copy, descriptions, form fields and metadata.
- Bold `700`: headings, navigation, button labels, totals and decision points.

No other weights are used.

## Tone of voice

**Clear:** say what happened and what to do next.  
**Calm:** warn without blame or alarm.  
**Accountable:** explain what is automatic and what needs owner approval.

Use direct labels tied to an outcome:

- Review order / مراجعة الطلب
- Confirm order / تأكيد الطلب
- Update ready stock / تحديث المخزون الجاهز
- Create today’s plan / إنشاء خطة اليوم
- See sales details / عرض تفاصيل المبيعات

Avoid vague labels such as “Submit,” “Continue,” “Do it,” or language that promises magic.

## Empty-state examples

**Nothing ready to sell yet**  
Record today’s finished items to see what customers can order now.

**No sales to read yet**  
Confirm the first order and your sales view will start here.

**Your day is clear**  
Capture a customer message when the next order arrives.

## Visual rules

- Use flat colour fields, solid borders and restrained shadows.
- Use line icons that describe a concrete object or action.
- Keep decision buttons specific and owner-controlled.
- Do not use gradients, glass effects, decorative blobs, sparkle or star icons, violet, indigo, emoji, or “AI-Powered” language.
